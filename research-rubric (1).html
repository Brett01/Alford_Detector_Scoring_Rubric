Point values and criteria are drawn directly from the Social Work Research I (SSW75100, Fall Semester) syllabus: Developing Your Topic (5), Introduction & Problem Statement (15), Literature Review (30), Research Methodology (10), Discussion: Limitations & Implications (10), Final Proposal Upload (10), Proposal Presentation (10, using the syllabus's own presentation criteria), Class Participation/Quizzes (5), and CITI Human Research Protection training (5, pass/fail at 80%+). Grade tiers: No Credit below 86, Credit 86–95, Honors 96–100. Each section's text can be pasted in directly or pulled from an uploaded .docx (Word document text extraction happens in your browser — the file itself is never uploaded anywhere). Each section also accepts an optional reference template or model example, which the AI checks the student's work against for structure and completeness alongside the rubric criteria. "Suggest scores from this text" sends the pasted submission and rubric criteria to Claude for a starting score and rationale per criterion — always reviewed by you before it counts, and easy to override with the level buttons or the manual score field. This tool runs entirely in your browser — nothing is saved once you close the page, so export the roster to CSV to keep a copy.
